02 Submit a bot
Use the leaderboard uploader for a binary .bin file or a hexadecimal .hex file. The server validates the program;
changing a filename does not convert an invalid file into a valid bot.
Test locally with the supplied executor before uploading.
A valid upload creates a new revision. Watch its status on the leaderboard; a successful upload does not mean its
results are already published.

After evaluation completes, the pending revision becomes active and the previous active revision is retired. Until
then, your previous active bot remains in use. Revision contents are immutable: improving the bot means
submitting another revision.
You can have only one pending revision at a time. Wait for its evaluation to finish before uploading another.
Submissions also follow the current cooldown, deadline, and any organizer pause. Follow the uploader's status
and the server's error message rather than repeatedly resubmitting.

03 Read the results
Live evaluation is published in completed sweeps. A sweep becomes visible together, rather than exposing
partially updated results while it runs.
A pending revision and an unchanged table do not, by themselves, mean that your upload failed. Check the
revision status first.
They summarize your evaluated results. Games includes wins, ties, and losses; a draw does not count as a win
for the CTF points formula.
The live table keeps the newest result for each team pair. It is not a lifetime total of every match you have ever
played, so standings can change as bots and opponents change.
More wins comes first, then more ties, then fewer losses. If those are equal, the earlier active submission comes
first, followed by team ID in ascending order.

04 CTF points
Your points are based on your win rate, with a small deduction to order equal win rates. The scored players must
have equal game totals.
win rate = wins / games
adjusted rate = win rate − tie deduction
points = 100 + 4900 × adjusted rate
For a player on the scored board, zero wins earns 100 points. A perfect record with no deduction earns 5000.
With no games, the win rate is treated as zero.
The current leader is not automatically awarded 5000: scores are not scaled against the best bot.

Within each equal-win-rate group, players are ordered by leaderboard rank. Their tie positions start at 0, then 1,
2, and so on.
deduction = min(win rate, tie position × 0.0004)
The first player loses nothing. Each next position deducts another 1.96 points, stopping at the 100-point floor. For
example, two players with a 50% win rate receive 2550.00 and 2548.04 before platform rounding.
The CTF platform receives points / 5000, rounded half-up to four decimal places. On a 5000-point challenge,
this gives half-point steps: 2548.04 becomes 2548.00.
Score updates are published separately from the live leaderboard. A delay in the platform display does not
change the scoring formula.
05 Troubleshooting
Authentication: use your Capture The System Submission Token, without extra spaces.
Invalid program or format: check the uploaded file, and test it locally. For API uploads, check the content
type too.
Evaluation pending: wait for your current pending revision to finish.
Duplicate bot: the replacement must differ from your active bot.
Cooldown: wait for the stated retry time.
Paused or closed: check the submission status and organizer announcements.
